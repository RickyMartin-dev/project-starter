Project Starter / Operating System

A second brain that ships.

One repository-local contract for Codex, Claude Code, Cursor, and future agents: recoverable context, evidence-led learning, fresh research, safe specialist routing, and security checks that travel with the project.

portablereviewabledomain-agnostic
01 / Continuity

Compaction becomes a checkpoint.

`./scripts/project compact slug` captures branch, commit, inventory, project facts, active work, risks, research, and verification paths. The agent adds rationale and next actions. Nothing important lives only in chat.

Facts

Git state, file map, commands, architecture, and current task.

Meaning

Decisions, trade-offs, uncertainty, and failed approaches.

Resume

One safe next action with evidence another agent can reproduce.

02 / Improvement

Research turns into a roadmap.

Fresh comparison

Research reports record sources, dates, limitations, adoption decisions, and recheck triggers. External ideas are input, never repository authority.

Upgrade suggestions

Deterministic signals surface placeholders, stale compactions, missing research, and unreviewed proposals. Suggestions require evidence before promotion.

03 / Delegation

Specialists are role cards before runtimes.

1

Define

Mission, inputs, outputs, authority, credentials, evidence, and rollback.

2

Review

Owner or designated reviewer approves the bounded role brief.

3

Delegate

Codex, Claude, Cursor, or another harness may execute the role inside the project's declared boundaries.
